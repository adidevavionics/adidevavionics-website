Company overview

Adidev Avionics Private Limited

Adidev Avionics Private Limited is a newly incorporated company based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. We are established to work in defence electronics, aerospace, avionics, RF engineering and embedded systems, together with electronics design and manufacturing.

As a new company, we are building our capabilities with a clear purpose: to offer organisations a focused, dependable engineering partner. We believe that credibility is earned through quality of work, so this website describes what we do and aim to do, and it will grow as our work grows.

Our Vision

To be a trusted engineering partner for defence, aerospace and industrial electronics, recognised for precision, reliability and the quality of our work.

Our Mission

To design, develop and support dependable electronic systems, working closely with our customers and holding every project to a high standard of engineering discipline.

Engineering philosophy

How we approach engineering

  • Understand first. We start by clearly understanding requirements, constraints and operating conditions.
  • Design with verification in mind. Testability and traceability are considered from the beginning, not added at the end.
  • Document clearly. Good records make systems easier to review, maintain and improve.
  • Test thoroughly. We verify against requirements before anything is handed over.
  • Communicate openly. Customers should always know where a project stands.
