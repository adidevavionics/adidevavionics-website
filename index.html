Who we are

A new engineering company built on precision

Adidev Avionics Private Limited is a newly incorporated electronics engineering company based in Hyderabad, Telangana. We are being built to design, develop and support electronic systems for defence, aerospace and industrial applications.

Our focus is on careful engineering, clear documentation and dependable delivery. We welcome conversations with organisations that need a focused engineering partner.
