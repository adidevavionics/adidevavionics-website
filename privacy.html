Privacy Policy

How Adidev Avionics Private Limited handles personal information submitted through this website.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Adidev Avionics Private Limited, a company incorporated in India, with its registered office in Hyderabad, Telangana. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to the company.

2. What information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to send us through the enquiry and careers forms:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your organisation (optional)
  • The category of your enquiry and the message you write
  • A link to your resume or profile, if you choose to include one (careers form only)

We do not ask for file uploads, payment details or government identity numbers. Please do not include classified, export-controlled or other sensitive information in any form.

3. How we use your information

  • To read and respond to your enquiry
  • To consider your interest in careers or internships
  • To keep a record of business communications

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

4. Legal basis

We process the information you submit on the basis of your consent, which you give by submitting a form. This approach is intended to be consistent with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, together with rules made under them.

5. How forms are processed

Form submissions are delivered to us by a third-party form-handling service, which passes your message to our business email. That service therefore processes your submission on our behalf. Its own privacy terms apply to its handling of that data.

To be confirmed
Name of the form service and its privacy policy link will be inserted once the service is connected. Data may be processed on servers outside India.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or tracking tools. Your browser may store small technical data needed to display pages.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your information only for as long as it is needed to respond to you and to maintain reasonable business records, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

8. Security

This website is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to give you access to the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to erase it, or to withdraw your consent. You may also raise a grievance with us. To do so, write to the contact point below. If you remain unsatisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational and its procedures apply.

This website may link to other sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

11. Children

This website is intended for business and professional visitors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page, with its date.
